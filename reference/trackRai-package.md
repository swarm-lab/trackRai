# trackRai: Multi-Object Tracking With YOLO11

trackRai is a YOLO-based multi-object tracking software for R. It
provides a series of Shiny apps for automating the preparation and
training of YOLO11 models, for performing the tracking of multiple
objects in a video, and for visualizing the results of the tracking
process.
